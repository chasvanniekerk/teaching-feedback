Your feedback will help me understand which teaching practices you recognise in my lessons and where I can improve.

I will use the results to reflect on and improve my teaching. They will help me understand what is already working well and identify areas where I can develop further.

The 12 principles

These are the teaching principles you will encounter in the questionnaire.

#PrincipleWhat this means in my teaching
1Activate prior knowledgeI help students learn new things by connecting them to knowledge and experiences they already have.
2Give clear instructionI explain new material clearly and break difficult ideas into manageable steps.
3Use examplesI use examples to help students understand new or difficult concepts and show how they can be applied.
4Combine words and visualsI use relevant diagrams, images, models or other visuals alongside my explanations to make ideas easier to understand.
5Make students think activelyI use questions and activities that require students to think about and work with what they are learning.
6Check understandingI regularly check whether students understand the material before I move on.
7Support difficult tasksI give students guidance and support when they are learning something difficult and gradually reduce that support as they become more confident.
8Spread practice over timeI return to important knowledge and skills in later lessons so students have opportunities to practise them again.
9Vary practiceI give students different types of exercises and situations in which to practise and apply what they have learned.
10Use testing as learningI use questions, quizzes or other activities that encourage students to recall what they have learned from memory.
11Give feedback that makes students thinkI give feedback that helps students think about their work, recognise what they can improve and decide what to do next.
12Teach students how to learnI help students develop strategies for studying, planning their learning and evaluating their own progress.
