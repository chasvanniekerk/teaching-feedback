SPARKS Learning Preference Survey

You will see 20 learning scenarios. For each one, choose only the approaches you would genuinely use. Your choices can help you notice both your overall preferences and whether they change across different Learning Situations.

Model in development

SPARKS is a model in development. Its concepts, questionnaire and results are being explored and refined. The model has not yet been scientifically validated, and results should be interpreted as indications of learning preferences, not as diagnoses or fixed learning styles.

Rate each approach from 1 to 6 based on how likely you would be to choose it. 1 = very unlikely to choose and 6 = very likely to choose. You may leave an approach blank if you would not use it, and you may give the same score to more than one approach.
