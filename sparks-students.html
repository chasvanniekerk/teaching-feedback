Six ways to learn

SPARKS distinguishes receptive (VLR) and productive (DWK) strategies. Both can be useful; the choice also depends on the learning objective.

V — Visual: observe diagrams and images
L — Listening: hear explanations
R — Reading: study texts
D — Discussing: exchange ideas
W — Writing: express ideas in writing
K — Doing: try and apply things yourself

Five learning situations

  • Acquiring: learn new information
  • Understanding: make connections
  • Applying: use knowledge
  • Problem solving: find causes and solutions
  • Transferring: use knowledge in another context

Use these concepts to make more deliberate choices about learning activities.
