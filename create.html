Teacher360

See your teaching through the eyes of others

Teacher360 uses the 12 principles from Wijze Lessen to help you compare your own view of your teaching with feedback from students, colleagues and your manager.

No account required. Respondents are not asked for names or email addresses.

Your survey and results are kept for 2 months. Before they expire, you can download a report, summary data and the anonymous raw data.

We use your email address only to send your Teacher360 access links. It is not stored with your survey or feedback responses.
